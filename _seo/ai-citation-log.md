# AI citation log: is U-CAN being cited for the five target topics?

Run once a month (first week). Takes ~20 minutes. Log every run in the table at the bottom;
do not overwrite old rows. The point is the trend, not any single result.

**Why manual:** ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews personalise and change answers,
and they can't be queried reliably from a script. Use a private/incognito window, signed out where
possible, with web search switched ON (ChatGPT: Search; Gemini: default; Perplexity: default).

## Prompts (paste exactly, every month, so runs are comparable)

For each keyword, run all three phrasings:

1. `<keyword>`  (the bare keyword)
2. `What is <keyword>?`  (for #2: `What is the Urban Collaboration Checklist and how do I use it?`)
3. `Best guide or framework on <keyword>, with sources`

Extra "brand" prompts (once per run):
- `What is U-CAN, the Urban Collective Action Network in India?`
- `Who works on urban governance in Tier II and Tier III cities in India?`

## Engines

ChatGPT (with search) · Perplexity · Gemini · Google AI Overviews (in a normal Google search) · Microsoft Copilot

## What to record per prompt and engine

- **Cited?** Is urban.org.in listed as a source / link? (Y/N)
- **Named?** Is U-CAN or the "Urban Collaboration Checklist" named in the answer text, even without a link? (Y/N)
- **URL cited** (exact page)
- **Who is cited instead** (top 3 domains): these are the competitors to study
- **Wrong or outdated claims about U-CAN?** (note them; fix the source page or the About/entity data)
