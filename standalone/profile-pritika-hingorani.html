About Pritika

Pritika Hingorani is the Chief Executive Officer at Artha India. In addition to her management responsibilities, she also leads Artha’s work on urbanisation and emerging cities. Pritika was a Research Fellow and Director at the IDFC Institute in Mumbai. Previously, she spent several years at the IDFC Policy Group where she worked on projects spanning affordable housing, water, sanitation and rural development. Prior to IDFC, Pritika spent three years as an Associate working across the International Trade, Financial Economics and Antitrust practices with Charles River Associates, in Washington DC and London.

Pritika has a Masters in City Planning from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. She has a dual Bachelors degree in Economics and Political Science (Honours) from Bryn Mawr College, with a minor in Peace and Conflict studies.
