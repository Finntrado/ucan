The network

Our Members

At the Urban Collective Action Network (U-CAN), we are proud to partner with a diverse group of organizations that share our vision and commitment to driving positive change in urban development. Together, we leverage our collective expertise, resources, and networks to tackle the most pressing challenges facing cities today.

Together

What membership makes possible

Membership is what turns eight independent practices into a network. It is the reason a question raised in one city can be tested in another, and why a method developed by one organisation does not have to be rebuilt from scratch by the next.

Members convene through City Mixers, collaborate through the Request for Collaboration, host U-CAN Fellows, and shape a shared reform agenda through the Urban Reforms Collective.
