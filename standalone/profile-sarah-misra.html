About Sarah

Sarah Misra is currently Senior Director of Fundraising & Partnerships at Reap Benefit. A Special Education professional with a double degree in English Literature and Education, she is also the Founder of a chain of Schools, Chrysalis High where she set up 5 inclusive K12 schools across Bangalore.

Before joining Reap Benefit she was the Chief Learning Officer at Hippocampus Learning Centres, where she headed the school division of 20 schools in Karnataka; empowering teachers and students in underserved communities. She finds joy in creating opportunities for children and youth to explore, learn and find themselves and has committed herself to removing the barriers to education and learning at the grassroots level.
