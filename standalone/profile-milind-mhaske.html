About Milind

Milind has anchored Praja since 2010 scaling it up to become a key national player in transforming urban governance across India. He helped set up some of its key projects: report cards for ranking elected representatives, training programs for elected representatives and Praja’s Urban Governance Index in 2020. He is a business management graduate from Mumbai University and brings more than two decades of experience in managing development sector projects.
